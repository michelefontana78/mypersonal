# Privacy Policy – MyPersonal

**Last updated: September 1, 2026**

MyPersonal is a personal health and training application designed to display and analyze health, activity, sleep, recovery, and workout data from services such as Apple Health and Oura.

## Health and Fitness Data

MyPersonal may access health and fitness information including:

- Sleep data
- Heart rate
- Resting heart rate
- Heart rate variability (HRV)
- Workouts and physical activity
- Recovery and readiness information
- Stress information
- Blood oxygen (SpO2) information
- Other health and wellness metrics made available by connected services

This information is used exclusively to provide the user with personal health, fitness, training, recovery, and performance insights.

## Oura Data

Oura data is accessed only after the user explicitly authorizes MyPersonal through Oura's OAuth authentication process.

MyPersonal only accesses Oura data for which the user has granted permission.

The user may revoke access to Oura data at any time through their Oura account.

## Apple Health Data

Access to Apple Health data requires explicit authorization from the user through Apple's HealthKit permission system.

Health information obtained through Apple Health is used only for health and fitness functionality within MyPersonal.

## Data Use

MyPersonal does not sell personal or health data.

MyPersonal does not use health data for advertising.

MyPersonal does not share health information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Health and fitness data is processed only for the purpose of providing the application's personal health, fitness, recovery, and training functionality.

## Medical Disclaimer

MyPersonal is intended for personal health, wellness, fitness, and training purposes only.

Information provided by MyPersonal does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and should not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
